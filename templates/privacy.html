Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 8, 2026

1. Data We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us when you create an account, such as your email address and password. Additionally, we collect the financial data you input (expenses, categories, amounts) to provide the core tracking functionality of the app.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively to personalize your expense tracking experience, generate your financial reports, and maintain your account security. We do not use your financial data for marketing purposes or share it with third-party advertisers.

Your data is stored securely on our servers. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal financial data with third-party services. Any third-party tools used for infrastructure (e.g., hosting) are bound by strict confidentiality agreements.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact us at privacy@spendly.example.com.
